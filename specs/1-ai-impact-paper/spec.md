# Feature Specification: Research paper on AI's impact on education

**Feature Branch**: `001-ai-impact-paper`  
**Created**: 2025-12-07  
**Status**: Draft  
**Input**: User description: "Research paper on AI's impact on education"

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Research AI's Impact on Education (Priority: P1)

A researcher needs to gather and synthesize information regarding the current and potential impacts of AI technologies on various aspects of the education sector (K-12, higher education, vocational training). This includes identifying key trends, challenges, and opportunities.

**Why this priority**: This is the core purpose of the research paper and fundamental to its creation.

**Independent Test**: The researcher can independently verify that sufficient, relevant information has been collected and organized to support the paper's arguments.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the topic "AI's impact on education", **When** the researcher performs comprehensive searches and literature reviews, **Then** a curated collection of relevant academic papers, articles, and reports is assembled.
2. **Given** the collected resources, **When** the researcher analyzes the content for key themes, arguments, and data points, **Then** an outline of the paper's main sections and supporting evidence is developed.

---

### User Story 2 - Analyze Ethical Considerations (Priority: P2)

The researcher needs to identify and critically evaluate the ethical implications arising from the integration of AI in education, such as data privacy, algorithmic bias, and human-AI interaction.

**Why this priority**: Ethical considerations are crucial for a comprehensive understanding of AI's impact and are often a significant focus in such research.

**Independent Test**: The researcher can independently assess whether a dedicated section on ethical implications has been thoroughly researched and presented.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the broad topic of AI in education, **When** the researcher focuses on ethical frameworks and potential societal consequences, **Then** a list of critical ethical concerns (e.g., bias, privacy, equity) is compiled.

---

### User Story 3 - Propose Future Directions (Priority: P3)

The researcher aims to suggest future research avenues, policy recommendations, or practical applications based on the findings of the paper.

**Why this priority**: Providing actionable insights and future perspectives adds significant value to the research paper beyond just analysis.

**Independent Test**: The researcher can verify that the proposed future directions are logically derived from the paper's findings and are well-articulated.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the complete analysis of AI's impact and ethical considerations, **When** the researcher synthesizes the findings, **Then** a set of well-reasoned recommendations for policy, practice, or further research is formulated.

---

### Edge Cases

- What happens when contradictory evidence or research findings are encountered? (Researcher must critically evaluate and present both sides.)
- How does the paper address rapidly evolving AI technologies that might become outdated quickly? (Paper should focus on foundational impacts and enduring principles, acknowledging the dynamic nature of AI development.)
